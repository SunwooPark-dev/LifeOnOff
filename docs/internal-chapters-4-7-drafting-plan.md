## Anti-duplication guardrails
- **Chapter 4** should define pre-prompt clarity, not become a prompt-writing tips chapter
- **Chapter 5** should establish workflow architecture, not repeat the practical workflows from Chapters 8–11 too concretely
- **Chapter 6** should define allocation logic and human ownership, not restate later planning/coordination chapters line for line
- **Chapter 7** should define standards and review discipline broadly, not become just another writing chapter
- All four chapters should reuse the manuscript’s existing vocabulary rather than inventing a conflicting meta-system
- Every abstract claim should end in a concrete rule, checklist, or decision question
- Keep Part II operational, not identity-level; save scarcity/career/machine-shaped language for Chapters 12–14
