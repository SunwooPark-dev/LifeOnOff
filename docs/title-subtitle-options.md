# Title & Subtitle Options — English AI Book

## Positioning anchor
Lead concept: **The High-Agency AI Worker**

The title options below are designed to preserve three commercial strengths from the archive:
- practical usefulness,
- intellectual credibility,
- human stakes.

---

## Tier A — Strongest current options

### 1. The High-Agency AI Worker
**Subtitle:** *How to Think, Work, and Create with AI Without Losing Your Edge*

**Why it works**
- Clear professional positioning
- Signals action and self-direction
- Balances utility with identity and judgment

**Risk**
- Slightly abstract if the reader does not immediately identify with “high-agency” language

---

### 2. Working Smart with AI
**Subtitle:** *Workflows, Judgment, and the New Rules of Knowledge Work*

**Why it works**
- Broad and accessible
- Commercially legible at a glance
- Easier for general business readers and publishers to parse quickly

**Risk**
- Less distinctive than the strongest branded option

---

### 3. Don’t Think Less
**Subtitle:** *How to Use AI for Speed, Clarity, and Better Decisions*

**Why it works**
- Sharp, memorable thesis title
- Directly addresses a major cultural fear around AI
- Strong for readers who want practical use without cognitive decline

**Risk**
- Slightly more provocative and less conventionally “business-book” in tone
